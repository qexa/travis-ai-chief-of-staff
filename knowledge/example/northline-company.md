# Northline Logistics Group: company knowledge (FICTIONAL DEMO)

## Company overview
Northline Logistics Group is a Nashville-based freight brokerage and third-party logistics company founded in 2011. We move truckload, LTL and temperature-controlled freight across the Southeast and Midwest for manufacturers, distributors and retailers. About 220 employees.

## Leadership
Jordan Hale is Chief Executive Officer. Marcus Reed is Chief Financial Officer. Lena Ortiz leads Communications. Press and interview requests go to Lena Ortiz.

## Office location and hours
Headquarters: 400 Commerce Street, Suite 900, Nashville, Tennessee 37219. Office hours are 8 AM to 6 PM Central, Monday through Friday. Dispatch operates 24/7. Visitor parking is in the Commerce Street garage; validate at reception.

## Services
Full truckload, less-than-truckload (LTL), refrigerated and temperature-controlled freight, dedicated fleet programs, warehousing and cross-dock in Nashville and Memphis, and freight audit. We do not handle international ocean freight or hazmat class 1 or 7.

## New customers

## Billing and invoices
Invoice, payment and accounts-payable questions are handled by the finance team led by Marcus Reed. Standard terms are net 30.
